Who we are

CYLO promotes a good quality of life for children and youth: physical, mental and intellectual, emotional, social, and moral and spiritual development.

Why we exist

CYLO was formed in response to growing social and economic challenges facing children and youth in many communities: school dropout, unemployment, lack of life skills, child abuse, poor parental guidance, drug abuse and limited opportunities for personal growth.

What we believe

Children and youth are valuable members of society. Investing in their growth, education and well-being builds a responsible and productive nation. We work with government institutions, local authorities, religious institutions, schools, parents and community stakeholders.

Vision

To promote holistic development of children and youth for a better, responsible society.

Mission

To empower children and youth through quality mentorship, inclusive education, and a proactive sense of family responsibility in society.

Our objectives

What we are working to achieve.

  • Raise awareness of social challenges affecting children and youth, including violence, drug abuse and unemployment.
  • Encourage youth participation in community development and leadership programs.
  • Protect children’s welfare through advocacy and partnership with local authorities.
  • Foster family responsibility through parenting education and community support.
  • Equip youth with essential life skills and entrepreneurship.
  • Support access to quality, inclusive education for vulnerable children and youth.
